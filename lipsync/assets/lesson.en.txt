Good morning. Today we will work on the sounds that give learners the most trouble.

First, listen to the difference between she and see, and between measure and mister. Those two are easy to confuse.

Now say this slowly: the young girl chose a yellow shirt, and she wore it to the village on Thursday.

Notice how your lips move. For shirt they push forward and round. For sister they stay wide and flat.

Let us count together: three, twelve, twenty-nine, forty.

Sarah loves the rhythm of these little sentences. Button, bottle, rhythm. Feel how the ending almost disappears.

Take five seconds. Breathe. Then read the whole paragraph aloud, and I will tell you what to fix.
